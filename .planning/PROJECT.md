# Project: Stay Buddy

## Vision
Stay Buddy is a highly polished, premium hospitality platform designed for the Bangladeshi market and beyond. It connects hotel seekers (tourists and local travelers) with property owners through a seamless, aesthetically pleasing, and technically robust interface.

## Goals
1. **Highly Polished MVP**: Delivering a "wow" factor through UI/UX excellence, focusing on micro-interactions and premium aesthetics.
2. **Operational Excellence**: Providing a powerful Command Center for both Site Admins and Hotel Owners to manage inventory, revenue, and guest relations.
3. **AI-Ready Architecture**: Maintaining a clean, modular structure that is easily understandable and extensible by both human developers and AI agents.
4. **Scalable Flexibility**: Allowing for future integrations of advanced features like real-time communication, expanded payment gateways, and internationalization.

## Target Audience
- **Guests**: Tourists and people looking for hotels at any location, seeking a trustworthy and beautiful booking experience.
- **Owners**: Hotel operators looking for professional tools to manage bookings, analytics, and staff.

## Tech Stack (Summary)
- **Frontend**: Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, MongoDB/Mongoose.
- **Infrastructure**: Clerk (Auth), Cloudinary (Media).
